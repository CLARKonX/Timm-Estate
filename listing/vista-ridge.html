About Property

Vista Ridge sits on a raised plot with sweeping views over the airport corridor, giving every principal room an uninterrupted outlook. The layout favours open-plan living, with the kitchen, dining, and lounge areas connected by wide sliding doors that fold fully back in the dry season.

The mansion was completed with energy independence in mind — a rooftop solar array and backup borehole mean the property runs comfortably off-grid for days at a time, without compromising on the finish of the interior.
