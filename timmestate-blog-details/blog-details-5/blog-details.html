The First-Time Buyer's Roadmap: From Search to Keys in 8 Steps

Dunamix • Aug 19, 2026 • 7 min read

Buying your first home doesn't have to be overwhelming, but most first-time buyers underestimate how many small decisions happen between browsing listings and getting the keys.

The process breaks down cleanly into eight stages: mortgage pre-approval, defining your must-haves, shortlisting neighborhoods, viewings, making an offer, legal due diligence, final inspection, and handover. Skipping the pre-approval step is the single most common mistake we see — it wastes weeks of viewing homes you can't actually afford.

The Eight Steps At A Glance

Legal due diligence is the stage buyers rush the most, and it's also where the most costly surprises hide — title verification and outstanding land charges should never be shortcut, however excited you are to move forward.

"Get your pre-approval first. Everything else about the search gets easier once you know your real number."

— Dunamix

Budget roughly six to ten weeks from offer acceptance to handover if legal checks go smoothly — longer if there are any title complications to resolve.
