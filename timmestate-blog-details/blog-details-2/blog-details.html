Interest Rates & Investment: Navigating the Current Climate

Dunamix • Aug 5, 2026 • 6 min read

The last two CBN monetary policy reviews have pushed mortgage rates upward, and that's changing how quickly buyers are moving on listings. Properties that would have sold within two weeks in early 2026 are now sitting closer to five, as buyers take longer to confirm financing.

That said, this isn't a slowdown in demand — it's a shift in urgency. Serious buyers are still active, but they're negotiating harder and asking for more flexible payment structures upfront, particularly on properties above the ₦50 million mark.

What This Means For Buyers

For sellers, this means pricing accurately from day one matters more than ever; overpricing in a rate-sensitive market leads to stagnant listings that eventually sell for less than a correctly priced one would have.

"In a rising-rate environment, the sellers who price realistically from day one are the ones who close fastest."

— Dunamix

For buyers, locking in a rate now rather than waiting for a hoped-for drop is generally the safer play — you can always refinance later, but you can't recover the time lost waiting on the sidelines.
