The Outdoor Oasis: Elevating Your Exterior Appraisal

Dunamix • Aug 5, 2026 • 6 min read

Outdoor living space used to be a nice-to-have on a listing sheet. In this year's appraisals, it's consistently one of the top three factors buyers cite when comparing two otherwise similar properties.

A well-maintained pool with a clean surround adds real, measurable value — but the bigger surprise in our data is how much a simple fire pit and seating area can move buyer sentiment during an evening viewing, when the space is lit and lived-in rather than staged and empty.

Where The ROI Actually Is

Landscaping matters more than people expect too. Mature planting along a boundary wall reads as privacy and permanence, both of which buyers associate with long-term value.

"An evening viewing with a lit fire pit closes more deals than any brochure we've printed."

— Dunamix

If you're preparing a home for listing, prioritize the pool surround and one usable seating area before anything decorative indoors — the exterior is what sets the emotional tone for the entire viewing.
