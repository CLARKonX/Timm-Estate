Mortgage Financing in 2026: Navigating Rates Like a Pro

Dunamix • Oct 7, 2026 • 6 min read

With shifting monetary policy reshaping lending conditions in Nigeria, timing your mortgage application matters more than ever — and so does understanding which rate structure actually fits your situation.

Fixed rates offer predictability and protection if rates continue climbing, but they typically start higher than variable offers. Variable rates can save money in a falling-rate environment, but they carry real risk if policy moves the other way.

Fixed vs Variable: What To Choose

Lenders are also increasingly weighing employment stability and existing debt-to-income ratio more heavily than in previous years, so getting your documentation in order before applying can meaningfully speed up approval.

"The best mortgage isn't the one with the lowest headline rate — it's the one that matches how long you actually plan to hold the property."

— Dunamix

If you plan to hold the property for more than seven years, a fixed rate generally offers better long-term protection. For shorter holds, a variable rate can work in your favor if you're comfortable with some uncertainty.
