Shortlet vs Long-Term Rentals: Which Strategy Maximizes Your ROI?

Dunamix • Aug 26, 2026 • 6 min read

Short-term rentals can yield noticeably higher monthly income on paper — in prime areas we're tracking, shortlet units are out-earning long-term leases by as much as 40% per month at full occupancy.

The catch is 'at full occupancy.' Shortlet income is far less predictable, management overhead is significantly higher, and local regulation on short-term letting is tightening in several districts we monitor.

Which Strategy Wins

Long-term rentals trade higher potential upside for stability — fixed income, lower turnover costs, and far less day-to-day management burden.

"Shortlet wins on best-case income. Long-term wins on the income you can actually plan a budget around."

— Dunamix

Our recommendation for most owners: if you can't dedicate active management time or hire a manager, long-term is the safer strategy despite the lower ceiling.
