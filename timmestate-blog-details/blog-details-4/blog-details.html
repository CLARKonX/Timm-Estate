Smart Homes in 2026: The Features Buyers Refuse to Compromise On

Dunamix • Aug 12, 2026 • 5 min read

Smart features have quietly moved from bonus to baseline expectation. Voice-activated lighting, app-controlled thermostats, and video doorbells are now assumed to be present on any listing above the mid-market range.

AI-driven security systems in particular have become a strong differentiator. Buyers are specifically asking about motion-based alerts and remote camera access during viewings, not just standard alarm wiring.

Features Worth The Investment

The features that carry a real premium, though, are the ones that integrate rather than bolt on — lighting and climate systems built into the wiring at construction stage, rather than retrofitted smart plugs added after the fact.

"Buyers aren't impressed by smart features anymore — they're disappointed by their absence."

— Dunamix

If you're preparing a property for sale, a professionally installed video doorbell and app-based thermostat are the two upgrades with the fastest payback relative to cost.
