Material Matters: Trends Shaping New Developments

Hammed Timothy • Aug 5, 2026 • 6 min read

Developers are moving away from the glass-and-steel uniformity that defined the last decade. Across new builds we're touring this quarter, natural materials — reclaimed timber, limestone, raw clay plaster — are showing up not as accents but as the primary language of the facade.

Part of this is buyer-driven. Homeowners increasingly want a house that feels grounded and warm rather than showroom-sterile, and material choice is the fastest way to signal that. Part of it is also practical: locally sourced timber and stone often outperform imported glass systems on both cost and thermal efficiency in our climate.

What's Actually Selling

Wood-slat cladding paired with black steel window frames has become the single most requested exterior combination on our listings this year. Inside, buyers are asking for exposed structural beams, terrazzo flooring, and kitchens that lean matte rather than high-gloss.

"Material choice has become the new floor plan it's the first thing buyers judge a home by."

— Dunamix

If you're planning a pre-sale renovation, prioritize the entrance and kitchen backsplash — these are the two areas where a material upgrade has the most measurable effect on perceived value per our recent appraisals.
