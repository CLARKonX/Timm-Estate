The Hidden Costs of Buying a Home (And How to Budget for Them)

Dunamix • Sep 9, 2026 • 5 min read

The listing price is just the beginning. Legal fees, survey charges, agency commissions, and renovation surprises can quietly add 15–20% on top of your budgeted purchase price.

Legal fees and title perfection costs are the most commonly underestimated line item — buyers often budget for the agent's commission but forget that title documentation and government charges can run into the millions on higher-value properties.

The Full Checklist

Survey and structural inspection costs are smaller in absolute terms but are the ones most likely to be skipped by buyers trying to save money — which is exactly the wrong place to cut corners.

"The properties that go over budget almost never do so because of the purchase price — it's always the fees nobody budgeted for."

— Dunamix

Our advice: build a 20% buffer above your target purchase price before you start viewing, so a great property doesn't get away from you over a fee you hadn't planned for.
