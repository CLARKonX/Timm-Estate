Interior Design Trends for 2026: What Buyers Are Craving

Dunamix • Sep 16, 2026 • 6 min read

Warm, earthy tones have firmly replaced the stark all-white interiors that dominated listings a few years ago. Buyers are responding to spaces that feel lived-in and calm rather than clinical.

Biophilic design natural light, indoor planting, organic textures — keeps showing up as a top request in buyer feedback forms, particularly among younger buyers purchasing their first home.

Staging For The Modern Buyer

Multifunctional spaces are also driving buyer interest, with home offices that convert into guest rooms and kitchen islands that double as informal dining consistently outperforming traditionally single-purpose layouts in viewings.

"The best-performing listings this year aren't the biggest — they're the ones that feel the most flexible."

— Dunamix

If you're staging a home for sale, swapping cool white bulbs for warm-toned lighting and adding two or three large plants is the single cheapest upgrade with the most visible impact in listing photos.
